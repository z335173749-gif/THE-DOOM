Put your own audio files in this folder (use the exact names):

  ambient.mp3     looping background music (seamless-loop file recommended)
  enter.mp3       played once on first interaction
  transition.mp3  played when entering a major section
  hover.mp3       played when hovering a card
  click.mp3       played when clicking buttons / links

Missing files are fine: the site simply skips that sound.
Use only music and sounds you made or are licensed to use.
